KABHI PARTNER INFORMATION

Restaurant Partner Terms

By submitting a registration, you confirm that you are authorized to represent the restaurant and that your submission is accurate. Submission is subject to verification and KABHI Admin approval; it does not guarantee acceptance or customer visibility.

Restaurants remain responsible for their kitchens, food preparation, delivery staff and fulfilment. KABHI provides restaurant discovery, location-based ordering, order management and WhatsApp communication. Restaurant and menu details may require Admin review. A restaurant becomes eligible for customer ordering only after required setup and menu approval, and when service status, menu availability and delivery coverage permit.

Any delivery fee and order total applicable to a customer must be displayed before order confirmation. Accepted version: {{ partnerTermsVersion }}.

Privacy Policy

KABHI collects the restaurant, representative, contact, address, map, operating and delivery details supplied in this registration to verify and administer partner applications. Terms acceptance and submission time are recorded for audit purposes.

Application information is available to authorized KABHI Admin users. Only approved and configured customer-facing restaurant information is made available to customers. Contact KABHI to request information about your application or its handling.

Contact KABHI

For questions about a partner application, keep your Registration ID available. KABHI will contact the authorized representative using the WhatsApp number or email supplied in the registration when contact details are configured.

If you cannot access the registered WhatsApp number, ask KABHI Support for Admin-assisted recovery using your Registration ID and official business identity records. An Admin must verify those records through an independently confirmed business contact before the registered number can be changed; password recovery still requires a code sent to the updated number.

If your application requires correction, the Admin-provided reason and resubmission option will appear on your registration status page.

About KABHI

KABHI connects customers with local restaurants in Qatar through restaurant discovery, location-based ordering, order management and WhatsApp communication.

Restaurant partners continue to manage their own kitchens, food preparation, delivery staff and order fulfilment. KABHI keeps registration review, restaurant approval, menu approval and operating status separate.

Platform Policies

Partner applications, restaurant details and customer-facing menu changes are subject to KABHI Admin review. Approval of an application is separate from restaurant operating status. Restaurant registration alone never makes a restaurant customer-visible.

Restaurants manage food preparation, delivery staff and order fulfilment. Applicable delivery fees and final payable amounts must be visible to customers before they confirm orders.
